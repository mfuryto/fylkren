# Fylkren name and brand

The Fylkren name, logo and other distinctive branding identify the Fylkren product. THUNOLD-OLSEN (Norwegian organization number 996871126) is the identified brand owner. The software [LICENSE](LICENSE) governs source-code use; it does not grant ownership of the Fylkren product or brand. This brand guidance is separate from the software license and does not change it.

## Descriptive use is welcome

Third parties may accurately identify genuine Fylkren software or installations and describe compatible services. For example, it is appropriate to say that you deploy Fylkren, integrate with Fylkren, or offer independent consulting, training or support for Fylkren. You may use the name in ordinary text to explain those services and link to this repository.

Make your independent status clear. Do not suggest that your service, organization, modified software or publication is made by, sponsored by, certified by or officially endorsed by Fylkren unless you have written permission. Do not use the Fylkren name as the primary name of a competing or modified product, register confusingly similar names/domains, or present the official logo as your own identity. Use of the logo outside an unmodified, genuine Fylkren installation requires separate written permission.

Redistribution does not authorize removal or concealment of product provenance. A redistributed modified build must identify itself as an unofficial modified version based on Fylkren by THUNOLD-OLSEN, identify its distributor and material changes, and retain the notices required by the software license. It must not be renamed or rebranded to present Fylkren or a substantially derived product as independently created by the distributor.

This guidance does not prevent honest comparison, criticism, discussion or other uses permitted by applicable law. For brand permissions or an official partnership, contact THUNOLD-OLSEN through the repository's published maintainer contact. This policy is subject to legal review before public release.
